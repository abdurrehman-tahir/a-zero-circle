# Content Safety Notes

Use “halal-aware” or “halal-conscious”, not “all products are halal”, unless product-level certificates are available.

Use “selected for Pakistani skin needs”, not “safe for Pakistani DNA”.

Avoid:
- 100% safe
- all products are halal
- dermatologist-approved, unless verified
- chemical-free
- whitening

Prefer:
- glow
- clarity
- barrier repair
- pigmentation support
- sunscreen comfort
- sensitive-skin support
- ingredient transparency
